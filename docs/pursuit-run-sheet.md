# Pursuit run sheet — operating order

**Drafted:** 2026-08-25. The gate from `docs/txdot-soq-gate-spec.md`, reordered
by the clock rather than by tier. Interactive version published as an artifact
(enter a deadline; the HUB notice date computes).

## The finding that drives the ordering

**The hardest deadline is not the submittal deadline.** A HUB subcontracting
plan must evidence written notice to >=3 actively certified HUBs and to
minority/women trade organisations **at least 7 working days before
submission**. Seven working days is 9-11 calendar days.

Computed against the two solicitations analysed:

| Solicitation | Window | Questions deadline | HUB notices out by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 601CT0000003362 | 21 days | day 11 | **day 12** (9 calendar days before) |
| 601CT0000003400 | 14 days | day 6 | **day 5** (9 calendar days before) |

On a 14-day window the notices must go out by **day 5**. Miss it and the plan
cannot be made in good faith; a plan failing good faith is *rejected without
further evaluation* — the entire submission, unread. Nothing in the
solicitation's own ordering signals this; it sits deep in the HUB section after
everything that feels more urgent.

## Phases

**Day 0 — read and extract.** Deadline (date *and time*); goal programme
(DBE vs HUB); minimum self-performance (read, never assume — 30% on both recent,
55% on an older one); standard categories with percentages; NLC minimums
**verbatim**; major categories; preclusion status; questions deadline.

**Day 0-1 — can this firm bid at all?** Tier 1. Firm Active status; admin
qualification if required; exact legal names across three systems; preclusion
against the corporate family; conflict / E-Verify / revolving door. Cheapest
possible no.

**Day 1-2 — can we name a qualified team?** Tier 2. A named task leader per
standard category **who personally holds it** (not team-collective coverage); a
named leader per NLC tested against the prose minimums; PM is TX PE +
precertified + employed by the prime; PM not already named on another live
pursuit (one interview only — a cross-pipeline check); major-category leaders
free on the likely interview dates.

**Day 1-3 (or by the computed date) — start the HUB clock.** State
solicitations only. Divide into lots; notify >=3 certified HUBs per area in
writing with scope/plans/bonding/qualifications/contact; notify trade
organisations; prepare the justification if a non-HUB sub is chosen.

**Day 3-5 — compose.** Percentages total 100; prime >= minimum; goal met
counting only certifications in the matching service code; every category still
has its qualified leader after percentages moved.

**By the questions deadline — ask in writing.** Standard subject line, official
channel, only chance. Named selection-team members must not be contacted.
Answers are posted publicly. Watch for addenda through to the deadline.

**Day 5-12 — write.** Answers inside the allotted space (overflow is not
rejected, it is *not read*); NLC template as an evidence table, not prose; team
composition form with exact legal names.

**Day 12-13 — assemble and check mechanics.** Every listed attachment and
nothing unlisted; cover page signed by the PM; HUB plan **current version** and
signed (TxDOT revises it; a stored copy goes stale silently — download fresh
every time); file naming and limits; re-check every date against the deadline,
and flag certifications expiring *during* the contract term.

**Day 13, not day 14 — submit.** Fill every requested slot; **finalise** the
submission (uploading is not submitting — files in a draft when the portal locks
are not a response); keep the confirmation.

## Caveats

- The HUB notice date counts back 7 working days excluding weekends only.
  **State holidays are not accounted for** — move earlier if the window spans
  one.
- Day numbers assume the posting date. Found late, the calendar dates hold and
  the day numbers do not.
